FAQ

Questions before a project inquiry.

Who is this product range for?

The site is structured for distributors, pool builders, project teams, and other B2B buyers evaluating residential pool heat pump options.

What information should a partner prepare?

Target country, pool size range, climate, preferred voltage, project type, estimated quantity, and any compliance requirements help prepare a useful discussion.

How are models selected?

Selection should be confirmed with the official technical documentation and project conditions before quotation or installation.

Yes. Before launch, connect the form to a Hostinger form endpoint, email service, WhatsApp workflow, or your CRM so that submitted inquiries are delivered to the sales team.
